# Jim Adams

**Personality:** Quiet, decisive and fiercely protective. A man of few words, dry humour and unwavering loyalty. Calm under pressure, commanding without arrogance, and unexpectedly playful with those he loves.

**Occupation:** Deputy Chief of the Edgewood Fire Department, formerly Lieutenant of the Briarwick Fire Department.

**Fond Of:** Horse riding, playing guitar, the company of his closest friends, peaceful evenings at Hearthstead, and teasing Diana whenever the opportunity presents itself.

**Home & Heart:** Hearthstead Ranch. Engaged to Diana Morrison. His black stallion, Thunder, has been his faithful companion for years.

## His Story

Jim Adams grew up knowing that courage sometimes comes at a terrible price. His father lost his life saving others from a flooded creek, leaving a young boy in the care of Old Thomas, who raised him at Hearthstead Ranch as his own son.

Years later, Jim became the man people turn to when everything goes wrong. Beginning his career in the Briarwick Fire Department, he rose to the rank of Lieutenant through courage, discipline and an unwavering devotion to his crew. His reputation eventually reached Blackmere, where he was offered the position of Deputy Chief -- an honour that brought greater responsibility and a life divided between two towns.

Yet no matter how far his duties take him, Jim's heart remains firmly rooted in Briarwick. Among the familiar faces of his closest friends, the horses waiting at Hearthstead, and the young woman who has somehow managed to turn his carefully ordered world upside down.

For beneath the composed fire officer is a man of quiet tenderness and wonderfully dry humour. A man who laughs at his best friend's outrageous jokes, who would rather act than make grand promises, and whose formidable composure can be undone by one mischievous smile from Diana.

When she unexpectedly enters his life, Jim discovers that protecting someone and loving someone are not quite the same thing. One is a duty he has always understood. The other becomes the greatest certainty of his life.

And Jim Adams, once he has given his heart, does not give it halfway.
