# Old Thomas Halloway

**Appearance:** 75 years old, with a weathered face, silver-grey hair, gentle eyes and hands shaped by decades of working with horses. His trusty walking stick is seldom far away.

**Personality:** Kind, wise, compassionate and wonderfully stubborn. A gentleman of firm principles, dry humour and endless patience -- except with foolishness and anyone who forgets the 'Old' in his name.

**Occupation:** Retired horse trader and owner of Hearthstead Ranch.

**Fond Of:** Morning coffee, his newspaper, horses, peaceful afternoons on the porch, unsolicited advice and the increasingly frequent company of a certain mischievous florist.

**Home & Heart:** Hearthstead Ranch. Widower of his beloved Adele. Raised Jim Adams as his own son, welcomed Diana as family, and shares a tender, flirtatious companionship with Mrs Elsie Bloom.

## His Story

There are people who build their lives around grand ambitions, and others who quietly become the reason someone else has a life worth living.

Old Thomas Halloway belongs to the latter.

Once a respected horse trader, he spent his younger years travelling the roads of Edgewood, learning that horses, much like people, respond better to patience than force. Time has slowed his steps, but neither his humour nor his stubbornness has surrendered to old age. And should anyone address him as simply 'Thomas', they will soon discover that the 'Old' is a title he considers thoroughly earned.

Life has given him both great love and great sorrow. After losing his beloved Adele, the old ranch grew quieter, but his heart never closed. Years earlier, when tragedy took the life of his friend Reid Adams, he had taken Reid's young son, Jim, into his home and raised him with a father's devotion.

Then, one day, a frightened young woman appeared on his doorstep, carrying little more than a backpack.

He didn't ask where she had come from. He simply invited her inside.

And somehow, Hearthstead began to fill with laughter again.

These days, Old Thomas can often be found on the porch, dispensing wisdom nobody asked for and pretending not to notice the affectionate glances between Jim and Diana. His heart may need a little more care than it once did, but there is still room for those he loves -- and perhaps for the lively Mrs Bloom, whose visits have become suspiciously frequent.

Old Thomas has never considered himself a remarkable man.

But ask Jim or Diana what home means to them, and somewhere in their answer, you'll find him.
