# Cal Weston

**Appearance:** 35 years old, 197 cm (6'6") tall. Broad-shouldered and muscular, with long dirty-blond hair tied in a low ponytail, a slight beard and an unmistakably mischievous grin.

**Personality:** Loud, charismatic, flirtatious and gloriously troublesome. Fearless, quick-witted and fiercely loyal, with a surprisingly perceptive heart beneath all that swagger.

**Occupation:** Lieutenant at the Briarwick Fire Department, promoted following Jim Adams's appointment as Deputy Chief. A highly capable firefighter who thrives in demanding rescues.

**Fond Of:** Tavern evenings, friendly competitions, outrageous jokes, drumming in the firehouse band and calling Jim 'Shorty'. Particularly fond of provoking Beatrice Elwood, despite knowing perfectly well she'll win.

**Home & Heart:** Briarwick. Jim's oldest and closest friend, and a devoted member of the firehouse brotherhood. Romantically involved with Bea Elwood, the one woman capable of silencing him with a single look.

## His Story

If laughter suddenly erupts from a quiet corner of Briarwick, there's a fair chance Cal Weston has something to do with it.

And if a chair has been overturned or Jim Adams is standing nearby looking thoroughly unimpressed, the chances are considerably higher.

Cal has never believed that life should be lived quietly.

Blessed with more confidence than most men know what to do with, he has spent much of his life turning ordinary afternoons into memorable adventures. His friendship with Jim began in childhood, and somewhere between boyhood mischief, scraped knuckles and years of firefighting, the two became something closer to brothers.

Where Jim is composed, Cal is a whirlwind. Yet behind his playful recklessness stands an exceptional firefighter. When danger comes, the jokes disappear, his instincts sharpen, and the man who was laughing a moment earlier becomes someone others can trust with their lives.

Cal loves his friends fiercely, even if he'd rather wrestle Jim into a headlock than admit anything sentimental. He notices more than his constant joking suggests, especially when someone he cares about is hurting.

And then there's Bea.

The sharp-tongued, immaculately organised mayor's assistant who somehow manages to bring Briarwick's most unruly firefighter to heel with nothing more than a raised eyebrow.

Naturally, Cal insists he's the one in charge.

Nobody believes him.

For all his noise, swagger and spectacular talent for causing trouble, Cal Weston possesses something far more valuable than charm: a heart that never abandons its own.
