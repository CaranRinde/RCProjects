# The riddles on the tickets. One per line:   - The riddle? | The answer.
# Add, change or remove lines as you like. They come out in a shuffled order.

- I have no beginning and no end, yet every point on me is the same distance from my heart. What am I? | A circle.
- A bat and a ball cost £1.10 together. The bat costs £1 more than the ball. How much is the ball? | 5p. (Not 10p: then the bat would be £1.10, and the two together £1.20.)
- How many times a day do the hands of a clock make a right angle? | 44 times: 22 times every twelve hours.
- I’m an odd number. Take away one letter and I become even. What am I? | Seven. Take away the s, and you have even.
- What has hands and a face, but can’t hold anything or smile? | A clock.
- Three times a number is the same as that number plus ten. What is the number? | 5. Three fives are 15, and 5 + 10 is 15.
- A clock takes 5 seconds to strike six. How long does it take to strike twelve? | 11 seconds. Six strikes have 5 gaps of a second; twelve strikes have 11.
- A farmer has 17 sheep. All but 9 run away. How many are left? | 9.
- Which three positive whole numbers give the same answer whether you add them or multiply them? | 1, 2 and 3. (1 + 2 + 3 = 6, and 1 × 2 × 3 = 6.)
- I’m a three-digit number. My tens digit is five more than my ones digit, and my hundreds digit is eight less than my tens digit. What am I? | 194.
- How can you add eight 8s to make 1,000? | 888 + 88 + 8 + 8 + 8 = 1,000.
- What is the angle between the hands of a clock at a quarter past three? | 7.5°. By 3:15 the hour hand has crept a quarter of the way towards the 4.
- A snail climbs 3 metres up a 10-metre well each day, and slips back 2 metres each night. On which day does it reach the top? | On the 8th day. After seven days and nights it is 7 metres up, and on the 8th day it climbs the last 3.
- On the trigonometric circle, at which angle between 0° and 360° are the sine and the cosine equal, and both negative? | 225°.
- What do you get if you divide the circumference of a pumpkin by its diameter? | Pumpkin pi.
- Why was six afraid of seven? | Because seven eight nine.
- If two’s company and three’s a crowd, what are four and five? | Nine.
- I’m the only number whose letters, written in English, are in alphabetical order. What am I? | Forty.
- How many times can you subtract 10 from 100? | Once. After that, you are subtracting it from 90.
- A drawer in the dark holds black socks and white socks. How many must you take out to be sure of a matching pair? | Three.
